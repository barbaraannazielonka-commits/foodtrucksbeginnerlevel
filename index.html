STEP 3

How much? How many?

We use How many with food we can count (one taco, two tacos). We use How much with food we cannot count (rice, juice) and with prices.

Examples

🌮
How many tacos do you want?tacos = countable
🧃
How much juice do you want?juice = uncountable
💵
How much is it?asking the price
🔢
Two burgers, please.answering "how many"

Grammar point: number vs amount

Countable nouns are things we can count one by one. They have a plural form: one taco, two tacos, three tacos. Uncountable nouns are things we cannot count one by one. They have no plural form: rice, juice, water.

🔢
the number of + countable nounthe number of burgers, the number of customers
⚖️
the amount of + uncountable nounthe amount of rice, the amount of juice
🌮
How many + countable nounHow many tacos?
🧃
How much + uncountable nounHow much juice?

Some food words are usually uncountable: rice, bread, ice cream, juice, water, cheese. To count them, we use a container word: a bottle of juice, a slice of pizza, a bowl of rice.
